Chrysler Australia

Bold. Colourful. Unmistakably Ours.

Chrysler Australia's Valiant wasn't just a car — it was a statement. Vitamin C orange, Hot Mustard yellow, and block-letter headlines that shouted from every billboard. Built at Tonsley Park, engineered for Australian roads.

The Valiant story spans six generations and countless variants — from the humble RV1 R Series to the fire-breathing Charger R/T E49 homologation special. Along the way, Australia gained the Hemi-6, the Wayfarer ute, the Drifter van, and a marketing language all its own.

This site celebrates every model, every engine, and every era of the Valiant legacy.
