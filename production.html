Key SA Facilities

Tonsley Park

Chrysler's main Australian assembly plant, located in the southern suburbs of Adelaide, South Australia.

Tonsley Park opened in 1964 and became the heart of Valiant production. Every major series — from the AP5 through to the final CM — rolled off these lines. At its peak, the plant employed thousands of South Australian workers.

The facility was more than an assembly plant. It was where Australian engineers adapted American designs for local conditions, created unique body styles like the Wayfarer ute, and developed the legendary Hemi-6 engine.

Lonsdale Foundry

Engine production hub for Chrysler Australia, located at Lonsdale in Adelaide's south.

The Lonsdale engine plant cast and machined blocks for the Slant-6, and later became the birthplace of the Hemi-6 — entirely designed and manufactured in Australia. The foundry's output powered not just Valiants but also the Sigma range that followed.

When Valiant production ended in 1981, the Lonsdale facility continued under Mitsubishi's ownership, producing engines for the Sigma and later Magna models.

Production Milestones

1962
R Series Begins

First Valiants assembled in Australia from CKD (completely knocked down) kits. Local content was initially low but grew rapidly.

1963
AP5 — First Local Body

The AP5 was the first Valiant with a body designed specifically for the Australian market, though based on US underpinnings.

1964
Tonsley Park Opens

New state-of-the-art assembly facility in Adelaide becomes the centre of Chrysler Australia's manufacturing operations.

1970
Hemi-6 Production

Australian-designed Hemi-6 engines begin production at Lonsdale, marking a new era of local engineering independence.

1971
Charger Launch

VH Charger production begins at Tonsley Park. E38 and E49 homologation specials follow in 1972.

1980
Chrysler → Mitsubishi

Mitsubishi Motors Australia takes over Chrysler's Australian operations. The Valiant name lives on briefly in the CM.

1981
End of the Line

The last CM Valiant rolls off the Tonsley Park line. Nearly two decades of Australian Valiant production comes to a close.
