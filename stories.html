Owner Stories

Tales from the people who lived the Valiant legend — owners, restorers, and enthusiasts.

MK
Mark K. · Adelaide, SA · VH Charger 770

"Dad bought a VH Charger 770 in Vitamin C when I was twelve. I remember the smell of the vinyl seats and the way the Hemi-6 would pull from nothing. He'd point at billboards saying 'Hey Charger!' and grin. That car's long gone, but I've got a CL 770 now — same orange, same feeling."

SL
Sandra L. · Geelong, VIC · VE Regal Safari

"Our family Valiant was a VE Regal Safari wagon in True Blue. Mum packed us three kids in the back for every school holiday — surfboards on the roof, esky in the boot. That wagon went everywhere. When I finally found another VE Safari years later, I cried in the carpark."

TR
Tony R. · Brisbane, QLD · AP6 Wayfarer

"The AP6 Wayfarer was my first car — paid $400 for it in 1985. Rust in the floors, but the Slant-6 never missed a beat. Hauled building materials, surfboards, and mates for years. Still got it, still runs. They're indestructible."

JP
James P. · Perth, WA · VG Pacer

"Restored a VG Pacer over three years. The Hemi-6 is the heart of that car — when you hear it fire up with those twin exhausts, you understand why people obsess over these engines. It's not just a car, it's Australian engineering at its best."

DW
David W. · Sydney, NSW · E49 (Restorer)

"I've been lucky enough to work on two E49s. One hundred and forty-nine built, and every one is different. The triple Webers, the blueprinted engine, the four-speed — it's all race car dressed as a road car. When you drive one, you understand why they command the prices they do."

AN
Anne N. · Hobart, TAS · CM Regal

"My CM Regal was the last Valiant my family ever bought new. Dad worked at the dealership in Hobart. When Chrysler became Mitsubishi, the name changed but the car was still unmistakably a Valiant. I kept it when Dad passed — it's how I remember him."
