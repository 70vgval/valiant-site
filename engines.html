Chrysler Hemi-6 (Australian)

Designed and built in Australia, the Hemi-6 was a straight-six with a hemispherical combustion chamber. Available in 215ci (3.5L), 245ci (4.0L), and 265ci (4.3L) displacements. The 265ci in E49 trim produced a staggering 302 bhp.

The Hemi-6 Story

When Chrysler Australia needed a performance engine free from US export restrictions, they designed their own.

Engineer Willem "Bill" Barre and his team created the Hemi-6 — a straight-six with hemispherical combustion chambers, released with the VG Valiant in 1970. It would become one of the greatest six-cylinder engines ever built, powering everything from family sedans to Bathurst-winning homologation specials.
