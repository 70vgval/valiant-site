The Chrysler Ad Style

Chrysler Australia's advertising was surprisingly modern for its era — clean geometric layouts, minimal clutter, and confident block typography.

Colours: Vitamin C orange, Hot Mustard yellow, True Blue metallic, and strong black accents dominated the palette.

Typography: Bold condensed sans-serif headlines in heavy uppercase — VALIANT, CHARGER, REGAL — paired with minimal body copy.

Tone: Confident, punchy, performance-focused. Famous slogans like "Hey Charger!" captured the spirit of the brand.

Motifs: Hemi-6 engine callouts, racing heritage references, and "Australian-built" pride messaging ran through every campaign.
